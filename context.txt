# Intro - Referral Marketplace Platform

## Product Overview
Intro is a marketplace platform that connects referral programs from businesses with consumers who have networks of people. The primary focus is on attracting consumers (members) to use the product and earn passive income through referrals.

## Core Value Proposition
- **For Members**: Turn your network into passive income by referring friends for jobs, products, and opportunities
- **For Companies**: Access qualified referrals from trusted networks
- **Tagline**: "You know people, we know opportunities"

## Design System
- **Background Color**: #f8f7f5 (warm off-white)
- **Text Color**: #1d1d1d (dark charcoal)
- **CTA Button Color**: #1d1d1d with #f8f7f5 text
- **Shadow Color**: #f9fd8f (bright yellow for cards, buttons, nav, footer)
- **Font**: Aileron
- **Style**: Round corners, gradient shadows, thin outer edges on cards
- **Animations**: Subtle, performant micro-interactions and hover states
